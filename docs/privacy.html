Lancar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Lancar is an offline Indonesian vocabulary trainer. It is built to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Lancar does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

Where your data lives

Your learning progress, settings, and the optional display name you may enter during onboarding are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Network

Lancar makes no network connections at runtime. All content and audio are bundled inside the app.

Microphone & speech

If you use the pronunciation-practice feature, Lancar uses your microphone and on-device speech recognition only to check what you say, on your device. Audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Children

Lancar contains no objectionable content and collects no data, so it is safe for all ages.

Contact

Questions? Email babin@axveer.com.

This policy applies to the Lancar iOS and Android apps.
