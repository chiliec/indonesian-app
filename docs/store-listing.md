## Full description (both stores)

Lancar — learn Indonesian vocabulary, anywhere.

Lancar is a fast, offline Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia) vocabulary trainer. No
account, no internet, no distractions — just you and ~2,000 essential words,
with native audio on thousands of cards.

How it works
• Browse 8 topic modules, or shuffle everything with Mixed.
• Answer quick 4-option drills in three modes: listen, read, and produce.
• Every word you get right is marked mastered — watch each module's progress climb.

Why Lancar
• 100% offline — all words and audio are bundled in the app.
• Native-audio pronunciation on thousands of cards.
• Clean, focused design — no streaks to babysit, no paywalls.
• Private by design — your progress stays on your device.

Selamat belajar! (Happy learning!)
